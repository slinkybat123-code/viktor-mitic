Series · Painting

Rain Dance

Paintings made outdoors, in the rain, at sites across North America.

The process

Weather as a collaborator

In Rain Dance, Mitic mixes natural elements with traditional painting mediums. Each canvas was made outside while it rained, so the weather on the day shaped the result as much as the brush did.

He came to the method by accident and calls it acid rain painting: non-representational paintings on canvas that the environment helps to make. He has said he enjoys rebuilding structures that nature has erased.

Galaxy (2008) was shown at Muramatsu Gallery in Tokyo and later sold at a charity auction in Toronto to a private collector. It is on the cover of the Rain Dance book.
