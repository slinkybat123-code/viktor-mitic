Edge Clipboard

Your clipboard, one swipe away.

Swipe out from a small handle on the edge of your screen. Everything you copied is there: text, links, codes and images. Tap Copy and paste it again.

Get it on Google Play Download APK

Free for Android 8.0 and later. Contains ads on the settings screen only. The APK is for phones without Google Play.

Swipe from the edge

The panel follows your finger. Swipe back and it closes. Hold the handle to move it.

Text and images

Saves every copy, keeps the original colours, and has no size limit for a clip.

Pin what matters

Touch and hold a clip to pin it. Pinned clips stay when old clips are deleted.

Adjust everything

Handle size, touch area, panel width, blur, text size and more, with a live preview.

Profiles

Save your settings as profiles. Export them and import them on a new phone.

Private

Your clips stay on your phone. Passwords marked as sensitive are never saved.

Install the APK

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Edge Clipboard and turn on Copy detection.
