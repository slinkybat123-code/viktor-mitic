# Privacy policy — Edge Clipboard (Android and iPhone)

Last update: 24 November 2026

## Data the app uses
- **Clipboard content (text and images).** The app saves items you copy so you can paste them again.
- **Copy events.** If you turn on Copy detection, the app uses the Android Accessibility Service API only to detect a tap on "Copy" or a "Copied" message. It does not read screen content and does not record what you type.

- **iPhone keyboard.** The Edge Clipboard keyboard asks for "Full Access" only to read the clips you saved. It does not record what you type and has no network access.

- **Ads.** The settings screen shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: the advertising ID, device and app information (such as model, Android version and app version), approximate location from the IP address, and ad interactions and diagnostics. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Diagnostics > Ad privacy choices, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Clips are never used for ads.** Ads are shown only on the settings screen, never in the clipboard panel, and clip content is never sent to Google or anyone else.

## Where the data stays
- All clips stay on your device, in private app storage.
- If you choose a folder in Settings > Storage location, the app also writes a copy of the clips to that folder.
- Clips and settings never leave the device. The app uses the internet only for the ads described above. It sends no data to the developer.
- Clips that Android marks as sensitive (for example passwords) are not saved.

## How to delete data
- Delete one clip: touch and hold the clip, then select Delete.
- Delete all clips: Settings > Storage > Delete all clips.
- Uninstall the app to delete all app data. Delete the chosen folder yourself if you used one.
