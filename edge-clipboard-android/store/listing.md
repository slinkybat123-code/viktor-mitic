## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Edge Clipboard keeps your clipboard history one swipe away.

Swipe out from a small handle on the edge of the screen. A clean, see-through panel follows your finger and shows everything you copied: text, links, codes and images. Tap Copy under a clip to use it again. Tap a clip to read the full text.

FEATURES
• Edge handle: swipe out to open, swipe back to close. The panel follows your finger.
• Text and images: saves both. Keeps the original text colours.
• Full clip view: tap a preview to read or select the full text, or see the full image.
• Pin clips: touch and hold a clip to pin or delete it. Pinned clips stay.
• No size limit for one clip. Keep 5 to 500 clips, or no limit.
• Auto-delete: remove unpinned clips after 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days.
• Fully adjustable: handle size, colour, side and position; panel width, opacity, blur and dim; clip text size, lines, spacing and corners; animation speed. A live preview shows each change.
• Light and dark theme.
• Optional copy of your clips in a folder you choose.
• Starts by itself after a phone restart. No permanent notification when Copy detection is on.

PRIVACY
• Your clips stay on your phone. They are never sent anywhere and never used for ads.
• Ads show only on the settings screen, never in the clipboard panel.
• Clips that Android marks as sensitive, such as passwords, are not saved.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Edge Clipboard uses the Accessibility Service API for its core feature. It detects when you tap "Copy" or when a "Copied" message shows, so it can save the clip at that moment. It also uses the service to show the edge handle and the clipboard panel over other apps. It does not read screen content, does not record what you type, and sends no data off your phone. The app asks for your consent before you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
