Dealers

Thirty-six portraits of Toronto's art dealers.

The idea

The artist promotes the dealer

Dealers is a series of portraits in Japanese ink on gold leaf. It opened at Odon Wagner Contemporary in Toronto on December 11, 2009, Mitic's first solo show with the gallery, together with a book of the same name.

The series turns the usual arrangement around. A dealer normally promotes the artist. Here the artist promotes the dealer.

Nine of the portraits are currently listed as available.
